About The Event

人工智慧於設計自動化與測試上的應用

AI in Design Automation and Test

人工智慧正深刻改變積體電路設計自動化（EDA）與測試的典範，透過機器學習、深度學習與生成式AI，大幅提升設計效率、驗證覆蓋率與測試品質，並有效降低開發成本與上市時程。本論壇聚焦AI技術在晶片設計自動化與測試領域的最新進展與產業實踐，涵蓋從前端設計優化、邏輯合成、實體設計、時序與功耗分析，到後端可測試性設計（DFT）、自動測試圖案產生（ATPG）、良率提升與失效分析等完整流程。

隨著先進製程持續微縮、晶片複雜度指數成長，以及AI晶片本身對高效能運算的迫切需求，傳統以規則與啟發式為主的設計與測試方法已面臨瓶頸。論壇將邀集學術研究機構、EDA工具開發商、晶圓製造廠、IC設計公司與測試設備供應商，共同分享AI驅動的設計方法學、模型訓練與資料應用、可靠性驗證，以及量產導入的實務經驗與挑戰。

Artificial intelligence is fundamentally reshaping the paradigms of electronic design automation (EDA) and testing. Through machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI, it significantly boosts design efficiency, verification coverage, and test quality while reducing development costs and time-to-market. This forum focuses on the latest advances and industry practices of AI in chip design automation and testing. Topics span the full flow—from front-end design optimization, logic synthesis, physical design, and timing/power analysis to back-end design-for-testability (DFT), automatic test pattern generation (ATPG), yield enhancement, and failure analysis.

As advanced process nodes continue to shrink and chip complexity grows exponentially—driven by the surging demand for high-performance AI chips—traditional rule-based and heuristic methods are reaching their limits. The forum will bring together academic institutions, EDA tool vendors, foundries, IC design houses, and test equipment suppliers to share AI-driven design methodologies, model training and data applications, reliability validation, and practical experiences in production deployment, while addressing key challenges along the way.
